About TwoWaysOut

Why it exists

Last fall, the founder's own apartment building, Olympia Place in Amherst, burned down. TwoWaysOut is built so students can ask the right fire-safety questions before they sign a lease, and so towns can see where problems are being reported.

What it does

For students

  • Reads a pasted listing or an uploaded lease and checks it for smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, two ways out of every bedroom, sprinklers, heating, and electrical.
  • Flags missing or worrying safety information.
  • Gives you specific questions to ask the landlord before you sign.
  • For Boston addresses, shows the building's fire-related city records.
  • Lets you report a problem anonymously.

For towns & cities

  • Lists buildings ranked by tenant reports and official fire-related records.
  • Shows the tenant score and the official score separately.
  • Filters by ZIP code and problem type.
  • Tracks inspection status for planning, without changing scores.

Data sources

Boston building records come from {{ data_sources }}. Owner names and owner mailing addresses are never read from these files. Tenant reports are anonymous and submitted through TwoWaysOut.

Limitations

  • TwoWaysOut is not an inspection. It can't tell you a unit is safe.
  • Listing analysis only covers what the text says. Check each item in person.
  • Boston building history is only shown for likely rentals. Other addresses get neighborhood context.
  • Address matching can miss addresses, so some buildings won't show records.
  • Simulated tenant reports on the town dashboard are labeled SIMULATED and are not real.
  • Rankings come only from tenant reports and official records.
