How scores work

  • Tenant score: each tenant report counts once, at its most serious problem. Severity runs from 5 (no second way out, no working smoke alarm) down to 1 (other problems), and a report's weight halves every {{ half_life }} days.
  • Official score: {{ open_points }} points per open fire-related violation plus {{ recent_points }} point per fire-related city record in the last 12 months, capped at {{ official_cap }}. Only shown for likely rental buildings.
  • Total: tenant score plus official score. Both parts are shown separately.
  • The ranking never uses building age, neighborhood, or demographics. Inspection status doesn't change scores.
